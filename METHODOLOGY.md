# DoomsdayWatch methodology — Phase 4 research dashboard candidate

**severity_v1 is a bounded severity index, not an event probability.** It does not estimate the chance or timing of war, nuclear use, or catastrophe. Numeric event forecasts remain withheld; withheld means unknown, not zero risk. No confidence percentage, accuracy total, market divergence, or doomsday-clock claim is enabled.

The global severity is `sum(component × configured positive weight) / sum(included weights)`, bounded 0–100. The public manifest states every included tracker value and weight, the actual denominator, and omitted inputs/weights as coverage gaps. Missing, nonnumeric, nonfinite, negative, or zero aggregation weights cannot contribute. An empty denominator produces null and `insufficient_evidence`, never zero or calm. A partial aggregate retains an insufficient-evidence coverage status. Bands are low [0,25), moderate [25,50), high [50,75), and severe [75,100]; they make no timing claim.

A tracker component requires a finite configured baseline in 0–100 plus at least one eligible evidence-linked directional signal with a valid nonzero configured signal weight, or an explicitly valid analyst override. Signal magnitude comes from configuration; explicit event direction supplies its sign. Multiple reports of one signal do not multiply its configured weight. Baselines and weights are policy settings, not calibrated empirical quantities. The component is clipped to 0–100. Attribution separates configured baseline, evidence-linked signed sum, decay, coupling, inherited context, analyst override, and final severity. `signal_delta` is exactly the evidence-linked signed sum, never the residual between an inherited score and baseline.

The candidate explicitly applies no autonomous decay (decay term 0). Recency is a 72-hour publication eligibility gate. When evidence becomes stale, measured coverage becomes unavailable; this is not observed de-escalation. Coupling is explicitly 0 in this version because no independently justified evidence coupling model is authorized. Legacy coupling settings and scores remain archived context and cannot contaminate measured severity. Introducing nonzero decay or coupling requires a separately specified, versioned contract.

Inherited values are `legacy_unreviewed`, with rationale, review and expiry unknown. Explicit overrides require a nonempty rationale, finite value in 0–100, review time no later than build time, and expiry after both review and build time. Invalid or expired overrides are disclosed but cannot authorize a component. Valid overrides remain visibly analyst-supplied; they do not mark supporting evidence as verified.

Evidence eligibility requires an actual body, explicit originating source attribution, explicit geographic relevance, a canonical safe HTTP(S) origin URL and immutable publication identity, valid publication and verification timestamps, and recency. Source lists, source dates, old `time`/`timestamp` review fields, headlines and inherited notes alone cannot qualify. Future or invalid evidence dates and unsafe source URLs are rejected. These are input gates, not an independent audit of the reporter's truthfulness. Scanned, retained and rejected occurrences, rejection reasons, distinct origin counts, and missing coverage are public.

Originating evidence IDs derive from canonical origin URL (tracking parameters removed) plus immutable publication identity. Report revisions have separate content hashes; supplied incident IDs remain separate, with unknown grouping left null. Syndication must identify the same origin; source occurrences cannot increase origin counts or signal weight. Conflicting directions within an origin revision group are contextual. Corrections remain in the local revision ledger and its explicit public report projection. No event identity or event timestamp is invented from text.

`event_at`, `published_at`, `collected_at`, `reviewed_at`, and `verified_at` stay distinct and null when unknown. Build time is separate. Timeline confirmation advances only for a qualifying, previously unseen evidence ID and its supplied actual verification time. Corrections, carry-forward and rebuilding cannot refresh it. Historic activation context may survive, but historic confirmation is separately archived and does not become current verification. Ambiguous and negated clauses are contextual; ceasefire violations and attacks despite ceasefire cannot become opposing evidence. Planned diplomacy and restored power remain context.

The old uncalibrated legacy escalation index and probability history remain locally archived, unchanged as source artifacts. severity_v1 starts a distinct series with a discontinuity and component/weight manifest. Content-addressed input snapshots preserve subsequent incoming versions without replacing the original archive. Legacy issued forecasts, resolutions and cumulative evaluation totals are not changed. The generator does not issue or resolve forecasts. Prospective operator issuance and append-only outcome review use the separate Phase3 authority described below.

Only the explicit public allowlist forms the publication bundle: dashboard, sanitized current JSON, the explicit incident archive and manifest, methodology and named local map assets. Private inputs, the raw ledger, legacy history and forecast archives are excluded. Generation is local; publication requires a separately reviewed, operator-authorized public-only release. Map markers are geographic reference points, not incident coordinates. A successful build is not evidence of source freshness or forecast validity. The accepted severity, event registry and outcome contracts remain unchanged. Phase5 application, controller, strict archive build and real browser gates were accepted. Release tooling still requires final independent review and controller activation.

## Prospective event registry and outcomes (Phase3)

Numeric event probabilities and calibration remain unavailable. `events_v1` registers only three narrow event definitions: a confirmed direct Russia/NATO member armed exchange with both sides firing; an Iranian nuclear explosive test; and complete commercial Hormuz transit closure in both directions for at least 24 continuous hours within the forecast window. Conventional Russia–Ukraine action is not a NATO-war event. All other trackers explicitly report `event_definition_unavailable`; mixed trackers do not inherit a nuclear-test definition.

Issuance is an explicit prospective operator action using the live UTC clock. Builds, severity updates and archive imports never issue forecasts. The first eligible issuance per defined event per ISO week is selected across all defined events, with 7d as the primary cohort and separate overlapping 24h/72h/30d cohorts. All issuance fields, definition versions and cohort memberships are immutable. The planned review load is 3 primary or 12 total horizon reviews/week, estimated at 6 hours/week across horizons; this is a capacity assumption, not a claim of staffing or completed review.

True outcomes require attributable qualifying source evidence of the defined event within the issuance/deadline window. False outcomes require a review after deadline and affirmative source coverage of the entire window. Missing news is not evidence of a negative outcome. Unresolved and ambiguous remain separate. Operator source verification is recorded metadata, not independent automated verification. Corrections append linked outcome versions and preserve prior evidence and decisions; identical replays never increment counts. Legacy circular resolutions, inflated totals and ambiguous identities remain quarantined and cannot become valid outcomes.

The predeclared policy contains no numeric probability or numeric baseline. Source-backed resolution counts, complete expired review backlog totals and valid numeric metric sample size are separate constructs. Numeric metric sample size remains zero, and Brier/calibration remain null; elapsed time and bare sample-count thresholds cannot unlock them. Future evaluation requires a new prospective numeric policy and a suitable independently assessed sample/design addressing selection and overlap.

Public JSON exposes each available definition/version/threshold, immutable horizon/cohort fields, null probability/baseline policy, valid-vs-legacy provenance, quarantine counts and complete backlog pagination metadata. The book, raw outcome evidence and historical score files remain private. The detailed issuance/review, source, persistence, and Phase4/Phase5 field contracts are in `docs/recovery/phase3-workflow.md` in the implementation repository; that private workflow document is not added to the publication allowlist.


## Research dashboard and report archive (Phase4)

Phase4 was explicitly accepted at `1367c94b34303c2e44d7b189f8cb4f9cc37d7932`, including the real responsive browser suite. Historical pending/browser-blocked recovery notes are superseded. Phase5 adds the briefing and market-context contract below; final independent review follows Phase6 release tooling.

The initial current JSON and embedded snapshot contain at most 20 recent reports. The generator splits the **full assessed provenance collection**, not the legacy first-ten `news_js` adapter. `data/incident_archive.json` contains every remaining current report and retained ledger revision absent from the current collection. Distinct occurrences, including duplicates, are preserved; archive loading does not imply independent corroboration. `data/incident_manifest.json` and the embedded manifest specify recent, archive, current-report, retained-revision and exact total counts, archive bytes and SHA-256. Both archive paths are fixed generator outputs; no private forecast archive or input-selected filesystem path is exported.

Recent ordering uses publication time where known, otherwise explicitly distinguished review/collection time, with unknown dates last. Review ordering does not imply new publication or event time. Search covers zone names, tracker IDs and report text. Publication-time filters exclude unknown dates except in the explicit unknown-date option. Search and filters cover only the loaded collection until the archive is requested; visible counts say so. Every page contains at most 20 matching reports, with exact continuation counts. Archive/schema/integrity failure preserves recent reports and provides retry; it never silently claims the complete archive was searched. SHA-256 checks consistency with the snapshot, not authenticity or verification of reports.

Every available safe HTTP(S) source/origin/correction URL is linked. The publication adapter restores exact original URLs, including query strings and fragments, when the raw retained input is available; this does not change Phase2's canonical identity rules. Active URL schemes and credential-bearing links are excluded. Prior revisions retain original source links; explicit supersedes relationships are shown, and opposing directions within one unsuperseded evidence identity are labeled contradictory. A multiple-revision group without an explicit supersedes relationship is labeled revision, not presumed corrected. Unknown identity, dates and revision status remain unknown. Raw report HTML is escaped and never executed.

The first panel shows at most three newly seen qualifying recent revisions when a prior publication is available; otherwise it shows recent qualifying evidence with the comparison marked unknown. Qualification remains an input attestation, not external verification. No qualifying developments is stated explicitly. New evidence counts and aggregate severity changes are separate: numeric current/prior published values can be changed or unchanged; absent or null comparisons remain unknown. These comparisons do not establish causation, event timing, or calm. Missing implications and near-term triggers are stated as missing. Dossier watch conditions come from supplied configuration and are explicitly distinguished from evidence-backed near-term predictions.

Build time, latest available publication time and latest verification time are separate. Freshness is measured against actual available publication/verification metadata across the complete report collection, not the age of the HTML. Publications or verifications older than 72 hours are labeled stale. Unknown publication timestamps remain unknown even when review/build time is recent. A successful JSON refresh does not verify evidence. Invalid/unavailable fetched JSON produces an explicit embedded-snapshot warning; if both are unavailable, the dashboard displays a fatal unavailable state with retry.

All 30 canonical tracker IDs have dossier buttons and geographic reference markers. Direct `?zone=` and `#zone=` links open dossiers; unknown IDs fail gracefully. Keyboard users can use map pan/zoom and the complete dossier list when tiles fail. The local Leaflet attribution and OpenStreetMap credit remain visible. External OpenStreetMap tiles require connectivity; report data, dossiers, forecasts and methodology are local public assets. Modal focus is contained while open, Escape closes it and the background is inert only while it is open.

The forecast panel selects any tracker and 24h, 72h, 7d or 30d explicitly. Each defined event exposes its threshold, source and resolution rules and actual registrations for the selected horizon. Missing specifications disclose their withholding reason. No ranking of arbitrary forecast ladder entries, calibrated probability or HIGH-confidence claim is generated.

## Incident briefings and market context (Phase5)

Briefings compare incident evidence with an explicitly accepted snapshot. Severity movement alone does not establish a development or an implication. Repeated reports and unchanged incidents are deduplicated; explicit corrections, retractions and meaningful verification upgrades remain reportable even when severity is unchanged. Previously published corrections remain in the audit trail. Without a prior accepted snapshot, the message describes an initial baseline, not a new escalation. Missing implications and next triggers remain explicitly unavailable. Public notes and links exclude operational details and private locations; the dashboard retains the full public change list and source-linked dossiers.

The existing three-hour scans assess material deltas. The 09:00 Asia/Amman slot is the documented default for a daily deep brief; no additional scheduled job or continuous monitoring is implied. Urgent qualification requires a new independently verified incident explicitly relevant to material escalation, with supporting sources. A severity band or an `IMMINENT` label is insufficient. Unchanged scans produce no routine message. Coverage degradation produces an explicit coverage message only when its state changes, not on every scan. Missing coverage never establishes calm.

Private research inputs, accepted comparison snapshots, staged message proposals and delivery acknowledgements have separate state. Dry-run does not accept, stage or acknowledge a message. Generation is not delivery; a delivery acknowledgement requires an external receipt. Persistent daily decisions prevent repeat daily selection across restarts. Source verification fields are supplied attestations: deterministic software checks do not independently establish the truth of an external publication. Local builds and proposals do not authorize channel delivery or deployment.

Markets describe prices of specific contracts. They are not severity measurements or validated forecasts from this project. A comparable contract must match the forecast's explicitly defined event, YES/NO semantics, actor/action/geographic scope and identical deadline/horizon using fresh actual contract metadata. Peace-event prices cannot be inverted into generic escalation, and unlike contracts cannot be averaged. The displayed context includes the original question, actual deadline, price labeled as a market price, metadata and source times, liquidity status and original safe market link. Unknown, invalid, stale, expired or mismatched inputs carry reasons and cannot silently become comparable. Remaining time is calculated from the actual UTC contract end; there is no fixed 250-day fallback.

Numeric divergence remains unavailable while forecast probabilities are withheld or unvalidated. Constant-hazard translation is disabled by default and cannot substitute for event/deadline matching or establish calibration. Market clients remain public and read-only; the workflow uses no financial credentials, wallets or trades. The publication pipeline and dashboard consume the gated market context rather than overriding it with a raw market cache.
